How Multi-bit Plane LSB Works

This technique embeds secret data across multiple bit planes instead of just the least significant bit. The algorithm sequentially fills complete bit planes, maximizing data capacity while maintaining imperceptibility.

Capacity Example

Key Advantages
  • Higher capacity than single-bit LSB
  • Sequential plane filling for optimization
  • Better distribution of embedding
  • Flexible bit plane selection
  • Scalable for different image sizes
  • Easy to implement and visualize
